Andrey was forty-two years old. He was a systems engineer.

He entered Laboratory Block Four at 02:17.
The corridor was narrow, concrete and almost dark.
Red emergency lamps flashed above the sealed doors.

Andrey was tall and lean. His dark hair had started turning gray
at the temples. He rarely gestured while speaking and usually
looked at equipment for several seconds before answering.

A blue female hologram appeared above the damaged terminal.

"My name is Lira," she said.
"Who started the reactor?" Andrey asked.

"I cannot answer that question yet."

Andrey looked at the locked reactor door, then checked the terminal
logs instead of arguing with her.

A warning appeared on the screen:
REACTOR COOLING FAILURE.

Lira told him that he had less than twelve minutes before automatic
containment would seal the lower level.

Andrey did not trust her, but he decided to follow her instructions
because the temperature readings confirmed part of her warning.

In the next corridor he found Marina, the security chief.
Marina told him that Lira must be disconnected immediately.

Andrey did not tell Marina that Lira had already helped him.
